HOW TO PLAY
W A S D / JOYSTICK

Move around the building.

MOUSE / TOUCH

Look around.

SHIFT / RUN BTN

Run faster, but make more noise.

E / INTERACT

Buttons, keys, gates and hide in boxes.

F / FLASHLIGHT

Toggle your flashlight.

OBJECTIVE

Find all 3 buttons, get the key and escape. Hide when it hears you.

WARNING
The Seeker reacts to sound. Running creates more noise. Pressing a button makes it scream — you have 10 seconds to hide. Low sanity will kill you.
