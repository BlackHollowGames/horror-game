THE SEEKER GUIDE // 001
HOW TO PLAY

You are trapped inside a large abandoned house. Three buttons are hidden somewhere inside. Find them, retrieve the key, and reach the gate.

01
W A S D

Move around the house on PC.

02
JOYSTICK

Move around the house on mobile.

03
MOUSE / TOUCH

Look around the environment.

04
SHIFT / RUN

Run faster, but create more noise.

05
E / INTERACT

Activate buttons, pick up items, open gates, and interact with objects.

06
F / FLASHLIGHT

Toggle your flashlight. Darkness can hide important things.

07
HIDE

Use hiding locations when the Seeker is close.

08
MAP

The map in the top-left shows the player and the Seeker.

OBJECTIVE
1
FIND BUTTON 1

Search the first section of the house.

2
FIND BUTTON 2

Explore deeper into the building.

3
FIND BUTTON 3

The final button may be far from the starting area.

4
GET THE KEY

Once all three buttons are activated, find the key.

5
ESCAPE

Take the key to the locked gate and get outside.

WARNING

The Seeker reacts to sound. Running creates more noise. Interacting with certain objects may also attract it.

Activating a button can trigger a loud signal. Be prepared to move or hide.
